Opening The File

Every Body Tells a Story. This One Screams.

LOGLINE: When a disgraced coroner investigates a string of horrific, unexplained fatalities in an isolated mountain town, he discovers victims are being targeted by a predatory entity heralded by an impossibly foul, otherworldly stench — and once that rancid odor hits your lungs, you have mere hours before it brutally claims your life.

Three years removed from the scandal that ended his career, coroner Silas Wren takes the only job left that will have him: filling in for the county examiner in Halloway's Gap, a mountain town of four hundred people quietly burying its dead faster than anyone wants to explain.

The Countdown

Every account collected so far follows the same clock. Once the smell reaches you, this is what's coming — and how little time is left to do anything about it.

0h
First Contact

A smell that doesn't belong to anything in nature. Faint. Wrong. Gone before you can name it.

1h
Denial

Headache, watering eyes. The smell seems to fade. You convince yourself it already has.

3h
Confirmation

It comes back stronger — and only you can smell it now. No one standing beside you notices a thing.

5h
Isolation

Disorientation. The smell stops being a smell and starts feeling like it's coming from inside you.

7h
Claimed

No account exists past this point. Every case file ends the same way — closed.

Field Notes

It Has No Name. Only A Smell.

"You won't see it coming. You'll just notice you're the only one who can still smell the room."

Nobody in Halloway's Gap has ever seen it and lived long enough to describe it clearly. What they agree on is the smell — rot and ozone and something sweetly wrong underneath, a stench that gets stronger the more attention you pay to it, as if it feeds on being noticed.

It doesn't stalk. It doesn't need to. Once the smell reaches you, you're already marked, and there is nowhere in Halloway's Gap far enough to outrun a scent that's already inside your chest.
